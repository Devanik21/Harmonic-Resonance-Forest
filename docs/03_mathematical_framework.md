## Resonance Equation

Ψ(x, p_i) = exp(-γ||x - p_i||²) · cos(ω||x - p_i|| + φ)

## Interpretation

- exp(-γ||x - p_i||²): spatial damping
- cos(ω||x - p_i|| + φ): harmonic resonance
- γ: locality control
- ω: frequency selector
- φ: phase shift
