# Mathematical Framework

## Resonance Equation

$$
\Psi(x,p_i)=e^{-\gamma||x-p_i||^2}
\cdot
\cos(\omega||x-p_i||+\phi)
$$

where

- γ = damping factor
- ω = frequency
- φ = phase
- pᵢ = training sample

---

## Energy Aggregation

Each class accumulates resonance energy.

Prediction is based on the maximum resonance energy.
